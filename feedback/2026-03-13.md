- When adding a new set, copying the previous weight works well (confirmed good).
- **Need a way to record body weight as a weight value.**
- **Body weight should be configurable** (user-defined value stored in settings).

## Exercise Variations

- **Need a way to adjust or manage exercise variations.**

## Workout Naming Consistency

- When creating a new workout plan, **attempt to reuse wording from prior workouts** to maintain consistency.

- Potentially **consolidate "workouts" and "history" into a single `workouts` concept** due to overlap between the two features.

## Special Weight Cases

- For **banded bodyweight workouts**, the **bands used are more important than the weight value**.
- May need a band/resistance modifier field rather than a numeric weight.

## UI / Data Entry

- When scrolling to the bottom of the workout list, the **keyboard hides the last items**, making data entry difficult.

## Future Ideas
